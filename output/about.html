About Operos

Who we are

Operos is an independent technology review and buying-guide website. We help people choose the right software and hardware for work, creativity, and everyday life.

What we publish

  • Buyers guides — "Best of" lists for headphones, keyboards, monitors, SSDs, webcams and more, so you can buy with confidence.
  • Software reviews & comparisons — honest takes on AI tools, productivity apps, design software and marketing platforms.
  • How-to guides — practical tutorials to get more out of the tools you already use.

How we make money

We keep our content free by earning commissions from some of the links on this site. When you buy through one of our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects our recommendations or rankings.

Our standards

  • We recommend tools and products we believe genuinely help our readers.
  • Our content is written for people in the decision phase — we focus on what actually matters: price, features, and real-world performance.
  • We update guides regularly so the information stays current.
